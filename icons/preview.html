App icon family

Debrief, Pitcrew, Spotter, Pitwall and Dyno. Chosen direction: Enamel: one saturated hue per app on a shared tile, one bold glyph per app, a faint diagonal livery sweep that runs across the whole family, and the same light from above. The alternative direction, Carbon, is at the bottom.

Tray and menu-bar icons

Each status has four tiles: the macOS template on a light and a dark menu bar, then the Windows icon on a light and a dark taskbar. Status badges sit in the bottom-right corner and cut a gap into the glyph. On Windows they are coloured, and each status also has its own shape (ring, triangle, cross, dot), so colour is never the only cue.

Palette and colour-vision check

One hue per app, chosen by searching OKLCH space for the set with the largest minimum OKLab distance under simulated protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia (Machado 2009, full severity). Minimum ΔE (OKLab ×100): normal 17.7, protan 11.6, deutan 13.6, tritan 10.7. The glyphs also differ in silhouette, so hue is never the only cue.

Alternative direction: Carbon

A graphite tile with the glyph lit in the app's hue and a coloured livery sweep. It is moody and premium at large sizes.

Why it was not chosen: all five tiles share one dark body, so at 16 to 32 px only a thin coloured glyph tells them apart, and on a dark Windows taskbar or in the dark Dock the tiles sink into the background. Enamel puts the identity hue on the whole tile, so it holds up at every size and on every background.
