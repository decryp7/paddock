# Addendum to the ADR 0038 amendment of 2026-11-10: the push gate automates the pre-review checklist

- Status: Note, 2026-11-10. Adds to [the accepted amendment](0038-2026-11-10.md) (D1–D8 unchanged); no new decision.
- Source: the user approved on 2026-11-10 a pre-review checklist for authors (process only, no ADR):
  `~/engineering-rules/checklists/pre-review.md` (engineering-rules `ca4124b`) and the lines in `.claude/agents/*.md`.

The checklist asks every author, before hand-back, for lint on every target OS (`cargo xwin clippy`, not only
`xwin check`), `cargo fmt --check` in every touched workspace, the project's conformance checks on the touched files,
lockfiles regenerated by cargo or npm and checked with `cargo metadata --locked`, a Linux container run, a revert of the
fix to show its test fails, a check that any touched hook or config still works, and a list of what was not run. The
mechanical items among them, conformance on the touched files, fmt, host and xwin clippy and the locked lockfile resolve,
are exactly steps 2 to 5 of `./dev push-gate` (A1), and commit-lint is step 1; once WP-1 to WP-5 land, those items
move from the author's memory into the gate and the agent hook (A2) and CI's preflight (A3), and WP-8 replaces them in
the agent definitions with "run `./dev push-gate`". The items that need judgement stay with the author and the reviewer:
the revert-the-fix check (ADR 0086 records it), the Linux run (A1b `./dev push-tests` and ER-9's container leg), the
check that a touched hook still acts (RT-4 disabled commit-msg without any gate noticing), and the hand-off's list of
what was not run.
