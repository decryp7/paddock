### First launch

{{AppName}} is not code-signed yet, so your system may warn the first time you open it.

- **macOS:** first drag {{AppName}} from the disk image to Applications and open it from there. The first time, macOS says "“{{AppName}}” Not Opened": choose **Done** (not Move to Trash). Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to "“{{AppName}}” was blocked to protect your Mac." and choose **Open Anyway**. macOS asks once more, "Open “{{AppName}}”?": choose **Open Anyway** again, then enter an administrator's name and password. You do this once; later starts open normally. If the button is missing, open {{AppName}} again first.
- **Windows:** if SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info > Run anyway**.
